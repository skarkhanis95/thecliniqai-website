Our Mission

Built for the doctors who care for India every day.

The CliniQ AI is the dedicated clinical operating system created specifically for solo practitioners, family physicians, and independent outpatient clinics across India.

Why We Started

In India, over 70% of outpatient healthcare happens in neighborhood clinics and independent consulting rooms. Yet, doctors running these practices are forced to choose between clumsy paper registers or bloated hospital software charging thousands per doctor every month.

A typical outpatient doctor spends hours every day manually writing paper prescriptions, flipping through physical patient files, replying to patient messages on personal WhatsApp, and handling missed front-desk calls during busy consultations.

We built The CliniQ AI to give independent doctors the calm, unified, digital practice they deserve — without per-seat penalties, locked tiers, or steep learning curves.

What We Believe

Technology in medicine should empower doctors, never substitute for their clinical judgment. Our platform automates administrative clutter — scheduling, token queues, e-prescriptions, and WhatsApp reminders — so healthcare practitioners can focus 100% on patient care.

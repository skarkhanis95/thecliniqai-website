Privacy Policy

Commitment to Patient & Doctor Trust: The CliniQ AI is a brand and practice management software platform owned and operated by Samar AI Solutions Private Limited ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of healthcare professionals, clinic staff, and patients, and handling personal information responsibly in alignment with applicable Indian information technology and data protection laws, as well as Meta Platform and WhatsApp Business Platform policies. We never sell, monetize, or broker patient health data.

1. Overview and Operational Roles

This Privacy Policy explains how Samar AI Solutions Private Limited collects, processes, stores, and protects personal data in connection with The CliniQ AI web platform, practice management software, voice reception interface, and WhatsApp communications.

Roles of the Parties:

  • Clinics and Medical Practitioners: Independent clinics, solo doctors, and outpatient practices ("Clinics") utilize The CliniQ AI as a software tool to manage clinical workflows. Clinics act as independent healthcare providers and data controllers/fiduciaries responsible for collecting patient information, establishing lawful grounds for care, and obtaining any necessary patient consents.
  • Samar AI Solutions Private Limited: As the developer and operator of The CliniQ AI, Samar AI Solutions Private Limited acts as a technical software provider and processor, handling clinic and patient information strictly on behalf of and under the operational instructions of the treating clinic.

2. Categories of Information Processed

Depending on platform configuration and usage, the following categories of information are processed:

  • Clinic & Doctor Account Information: Doctor name, medical registration credentials (NMC or State Medical Council number), clinic name, physical address, business email, phone number, and subscription billing details.
  • Patient Demographic & Registration Information: Patient full name, age, gender, contact mobile number (WhatsApp-enabled), appointment date and time, queue token number, and visit reason recorded by the clinic during registration or intake.
  • Clinical Encounter Records (Doctor-Authored): Consultation notes, vitals, symptoms, diagnosis notes, electronic prescriptions, diagnostic lab recommendations, and follow-up schedules recorded and authorized by the licensed doctor.
  • Communication Records & Delivery Metadata: Message dispatch timestamps, delivery receipts, read statuses, and transaction identifiers associated with clinic notifications sent via third-party messaging infrastructure.
  • AI Voice Reception Records (Where Optional Add-On is Enabled): Inbound phone call audio recordings, speech-to-text transcripts, and caller appointment preferences logged by the clinic's optional automated voice phone receptionist.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

Personal and health information is processed strictly for legitimate clinical, operational, and administrative purposes:

  • To enable clinics and outpatient staff to schedule consultations, maintain live token queues, and generate doctor-authorized electronic prescriptions.
  • To transmit automated clinic-related communications (such as appointment confirmations, appointment reminders, queue/token status updates, and digital prescription links) to patients on behalf of their consulting clinic.
  • To process clinic subscription payments, administer accounts, and generate GST-compliant commercial invoices.
  • To maintain technical audit trails, prevent unauthorized access, and ensure high platform availability and security.

4. WhatsApp Communications, Clinic Responsibilities & Patient Consent

The CliniQ AI integrates messaging functionality to facilitate patient communications via the WhatsApp Business Platform. The intended operating model is governed by the following disclosures:

  • Clinic Responsibility for Consent: Clinics using The CliniQ AI are responsible for obtaining appropriate patient consent before sending business-initiated WhatsApp communications. Consent may be collected during patient registration through the clinic's patient intake or registration workflow.
  • Transactional Nature of Communications: Patients may receive relevant transactional communications, such as appointment confirmations, appointment reminders, queue/token updates, electronic prescription documents, and other clinic-related notifications, depending on the services enabled by their clinic.
  • Informing Patients & Opt-Out Mechanisms: Patients must be informed about the nature of these communications and how they can opt out. Patients may opt out of automated WhatsApp messages at any time by replying "STOP" or "UNSUBSCRIBE" directly in the clinic's WhatsApp chat, or by submitting a request via our public Data Deletion page.
  • Enforcement of Opt-Outs: Clinics must respect opt-out requests and must not send WhatsApp messages to patients who have withdrawn the relevant consent.
  • No Unsolicited Marketing: Promotional communications, if offered in the future, must be handled separately with appropriate explicit consent and in accordance with applicable policies and laws. The messaging infrastructure must not be used for unsolicited promotional spam.
  • Third-Party Facilitation: WhatsApp messaging is facilitated through third-party service providers, including Meta Platforms and authorized WhatsApp Business API / Solution Providers.

5. Data Security & Administrative Safeguards

We maintain technical, administrative, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information:

  • Data Isolation: Clinic databases are architected with logical multi-tenant isolation, ensuring that each clinic's records remain strictly segregated and inaccessible to other clinics.
  • Data in Transit: All communications between user browsers and our servers are encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Data at Rest: Persistent application databases, electronic prescription archives, and backups are stored using AES-256 encryption at rest.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Clinic owners can assign granular access permissions to associate doctors, receptionists, and clinical staff to prevent unauthorized internal access.
  • Note on Encryption: While data is encrypted in transit and at rest, data is not end-to-end encrypted throughout the entire platform, as clinic doctors and authorized staff access records via the web application to conduct consultations and practice operations.

6. Medical Record Retention & NMC Statutory Exceptions

Under the regulations of the National Medical Commission (NMC) and applicable Indian healthcare laws, medical practitioners are legally required to maintain clinical records, patient charts, and prescription histories for a mandatory statutory retention period (typically a minimum of 3 years from the date of treatment).

Consequently, while patient contact information, communication permissions, and messaging subscriptions will be purged or suppressed immediately upon request, clinical consultation records required by treating doctors for statutory medical defense and legal compliance will be securely archived under the clinic's control in accordance with Indian healthcare regulations.

7. Patient & User Privacy Rights

Individuals whose personal information is processed through the platform have the right to:

  • Access: Request confirmation of whether personal data is being processed and obtain a summary of relevant information.
  • Correction: Request the correction of inaccurate or outdated personal details.
  • Deletion & Opt-Out: Request the erasure of personal information that is no longer required for legitimate clinical or legal purposes, or withdraw consent for automated messaging.
  • Grievance Redressal: Submit privacy concerns or complaints to our designated privacy desk.

To exercise any of these rights, users may visit our public Data Deletion page or contact us directly at admin@thecliniqai.com.

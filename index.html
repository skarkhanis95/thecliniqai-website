The ₹999/month plan covers your entire clinic: appointments, tokens, patient clinical records, drug database e-prescriptions, lab orders, billing, automated WhatsApp notifications, and AI visit notes. There is no restriction on patient numbers or records.

How does the 60-day free trial work?
You receive complete access to all features of the master platform for 60 full days. You do not need to provide a credit card or make any financial commitment. If you decide it isn't the right fit, you can cancel at any time with zero fee.
Is there a discount if we pay annually?
Yes! If you choose annual billing, you receive an immediate 10% discount, bringing the effective price down to ₹899/month per clinic.
Do I have to pay extra for staff or visiting consultants?
No! Unlike older software that charges per doctor login, The CliniQ AI charges a flat fee per clinic. You can create accounts for your partner doctors, visiting specialists, front-desk staff, and nurses without paying extra.
Does the AI prescribe medicines on its own?
Never. Medical decisions and prescriptions remain 100% under the licensed doctor’s control. The AI only assists with formatting doctor dictation and showing relevant dosage presets from your prior patterns. Nothing is ever saved or sent without the doctor explicitly clicking approve.
